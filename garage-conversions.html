A garage that stores boxes is wasted space. Vision Touch Ltd converts garages across Greater London into warm, fully usable rooms — home offices, gyms, snugs, playrooms or extra bedrooms — insulated, finished and integrated naturally with the rest of your home.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do I need planning permission to convert my garage?
Many garage conversions fall under Permitted Development as they use the existing structure, but it varies. We confirm exactly what’s needed for your property.
Will the converted room be warm enough?
Yes — we insulate the walls, floor and ceiling, address any damp and add heating so the room is comfortable all year.
Can you match the new room to the rest of the house?
We finish the conversion to blend with your home, including walls, flooring, windows, doors and decoration.
How long does a garage conversion take?
Most single-garage conversions take around 2–4 weeks depending on the specification and any structural changes.
