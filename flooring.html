Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What types of flooring do you install?
Engineered and solid wood, laminate, luxury vinyl tile (LVT) and floor tiling, including large-format tiles.
Do you prepare the subfloor first?
Always. We level and prepare the subfloor and fit appropriate underlay or moisture control — it’s essential for a lasting finish.
Can you remove and dispose of old flooring?
Yes — lifting and disposing of existing flooring can be included as part of the job.
Which flooring is best for a busy household?
LVT and quality laminate are excellent for high-traffic areas; we’ll recommend the best fit for each room and budget.
