Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Which areas of London do you cover?
We work across the whole of Greater London, including boroughs such as Wembley, Croydon, Ealing, Harrow, Enfield, Richmond and more.
Do you offer free, no-obligation quotes?
Yes. Tell us about your project by phone, WhatsApp, email or our quote form and we’ll provide a clear, no-obligation quote.
Do you handle planning and building regulations?
We guide you through planning and building control where required and coordinate the necessary drawings, calculations and approvals.
Do you work with property investors and developers?
Absolutely. Alongside homeowners, we deliver renovation and development projects for investors, landlords and developers.
Is your work guaranteed?
Our workmanship is backed by aftercare, and installed products carry their manufacturer warranties. Specifics are confirmed in your quote.
