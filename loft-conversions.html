Turn unused roof space into a beautiful bedroom, office or suite — adding space and value.

A loft conversion is one of the smartest ways to add a room — and significant value — without losing a single inch of garden. Vision Touch Ltd designs and builds dormer, hip-to-gable, Velux and L-shaped conversions across Greater London, turning dusty roof space into light-filled bedrooms, home offices and en-suite retreats.

In London, space is the most valuable thing a home can have. A well-built loft conversion can add a double bedroom and bathroom while improving how the whole house flows — typically delivering one of the strongest returns on investment of any home improvement.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do I need planning permission for a loft conversion?
Many loft conversions fall under Permitted Development, but dormers, conservation areas and flats often require planning permission. We advise on exactly what your project needs at the quote stage.
How long does a loft conversion take?
Most conversions take around 6–10 weeks on site depending on type and complexity. We give you a clear programme before work begins.
Will a loft conversion add value to my home?
Yes — adding a bedroom and bathroom in the loft is consistently one of the highest-return home improvements in Greater London.
Can you build an en-suite in the loft?
Absolutely. We regularly add en-suites and integrate the plumbing, ventilation and drainage as part of the build.
